A money, change and profit math game for kids.

Run a canteen, count change, collect trophies

Kantin Pintar by Raz is an educational game that teaches Malaysian money skills through running a school canteen. Players stock up in the morning, serve customer orders and count change accurately before time runs out.

  • Real Malaysian money: RM1, RM5, RM10, 50, 20, 10 and 5 sen
  • Exact change earns combos, coins and trophies
  • Menu cards with facts about Malaysian food
  • Six aprons for Raz, a trophy collection and a Prestige mode
  • Bilingual: Bahasa Melayu and English
  • Fully offline, no ads and no in-app purchases

For parents and teachers

The game builds addition and subtraction with money in a familiar food-stall setting. There are no accounts, no ads, no networking and no personal data collected. All progress stays on the device.

FAQ

Does the app need Internet?
No. All content is bundled and works offline.

Where is my progress saved?
On your device only. Deleting the app deletes progress.

Are there ads or purchases?
No ads and no in-app purchases.

How do I change language?
Tap the BM/EN button on the start screen.

How do I restart?
Use the Prestige option on the start screen, or reinstall the app.

Privacy Policy

Effective: 21 November 2026

Summary. Kantin Pintar by Raz does not collect, transmit or sell any personal data. The app works fully offline.

Data we collect. None. The app does not request your name, email, phone number, location, contacts, photos or advertising identifier. There are no third-party analytics, advertising or crash-reporting SDKs.

Data stored on your device. Game progress (day, coins, trophies, menu cards, aprons and language setting) is stored locally in the app’s storage. It is never sent to a server and is removed when the app is deleted.

Network. The app does not require an Internet connection and makes no requests to external servers.

Children. The app is safe for children. Because no data is collected, no personal information from children is processed. There are no ads and no in-app purchases.

Changes. If this policy changes, a new version will be published on this page with an updated effective date.
